TEMPEST (Transformative Explorations in Multi-Physics and Engineering of Scientific Turbulence) is led by Michigan State University and runs from September 2026 to August 2031 under NSF award 2535317. It brings together eight universities and three national laboratories to build predictive models of turbulence in multi-physics systems.
