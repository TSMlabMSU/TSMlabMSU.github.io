Associate Professor, Department of Mechanical Engineering, Michigan State University. MS degree (2011) and PhD degree (2015) from Queen's University, Canada. Prof. Yuan developed large-scale, high-fidelity numerical simulation methods for wall-bounded turbulent flows with complex boundary geometry. Her research goal is to "push the boundaries of physical understandings of complex, realistic turbulence, and to develop physics-based and empirical closures for a wide range of applications."
